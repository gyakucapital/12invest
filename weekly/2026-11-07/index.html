马股本周微升 0.98 点收 1,616.58 点；国行维持利率，KEB、MyDCD 新股认购出炉

FBM KLCI · 本周（11 月 2 日–11 月 6 日）
1,616.58
涨跌
+0.98（+0.06%）

周二银行股撑盘，周四国行维持利率后指数大涨 19.30 点至 1,635.86 点，但周五华尔街 AI 科技股急挫，马股随区域市场跌 1.17%，几乎回吐全部涨幅。全周大马指数仅比上周五的 1,615.60 点微升，外围不确定因素仍主导市场情绪。 来源: Focus Malaysia ↗

  1. IPO

    本周新股：KEB 认购 1.01 倍、MyDCD 获 36.46 倍超额认购，IOIPG REIT 等多只新股开卖

    KEB（每股 35 仙）公众部分认购 1.01 倍，11 月 16 日上 ACE；MyDCD（56 仙）公众部分超额认购 36.46 倍，11 月 17 日挂牌。IOIPG Malaysia REIT 以每单位 96 仙开卖，Yes Group（18 仙）与 Emits（16 仙）也先后推出招股书；Big Caring 与 Impact Capital 申购分别于 11 月 5 日和 6 日截止，本周无新股挂牌，SKA Capital 将于 11 月 11 日上市。

    国行维持隔夜政策利率于 2.75%

    PARAMON来源： The Edge ↗

12Invest Weekly Wrap · WEEKLY

KLCI edges up 0.98 points this week to 1,616.58; Bank Negara holds rates as KEB and MyDCD IPO results come in

Sat, 7 Nov 202612Invest
FBM KLCI · this week（11 月 2 日–11 月 6 日）
1,616.58
Change
+0.98（+0.06%）

Banks supported the index on Tuesday, and it jumped 19.30 points to 1,635.86 on Thursday after Bank Negara held rates, but a Wall Street AI tech rout on Friday sent Bursa down 1.17% with regional markets, erasing almost all of the gain. The KLCI ended the week barely above the previous Friday's 1,615.60, with external uncertainty still driving sentiment. Source: Focus Malaysia ↗

  1. IPO

    IPO round-up: KEB 1.01 times subscribed, MyDCD 36.46 times oversubscribed; IOIPG REIT among new offers

    KEB's public portion (35 sen a share) was 1.01 times subscribed ahead of its 16 November ACE Market listing, while MyDCD's (56 sen) was oversubscribed 36.46 times, with listing on 17 November. IOIPG Malaysia REIT opened its offer at 96 sen a unit, and Yes Group (18 sen) and Emits (16 sen) launched their prospectuses; applications for Big Caring and Impact Capital closed on 5 and 6 November. There were no debuts this week; SKA Capital lists on 11 November.

    KEBMYDCDIOIPGREITYESGROUPEMITSBCGIMPACTSKACAPSource: The Star ↗
  2. Policy

    Bank Negara holds the OPR at 2.75%

    At its final policy meeting of 2026 on Thursday, Bank Negara left the overnight policy rate unchanged, citing solid third-quarter growth and a resilient 2027 outlook, though higher energy costs are lifting inflation. OCBC expects a 25-basis-point hike to 3% at the 21 January 2027 meeting.

    Source: The Star ↗
  3. Global

    Wall Street AI rout sends Bursa down 1.17% on Friday

    US stocks had their worst day in 18 months on Thursday, with the Nasdaq down 5.5% and the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index off more than 12%, dragging regional markets lower. The KLCI fell 19.28 points to 1,616.58 on Friday, erasing almost all of Thursday's gain; tech stocks were down as much as 5.78%, led by UWC, Vitrox, Pentamaster and Inari.

    UWCVITROXPENTAINARISource: The Star ↗
  4. Contract

    SunCon wins RM920.8m of extra MEP works for a Johor data centre

    Sunway Construction secured additional mechanical, electrical and plumbing works for a data centre in Johor worth RM920.8 million, due for completion by the first quarter of 2028.

    SUNCONSource: The Edge ↗
  5. Contract

    GDB lands RM877m Shah Alam logistics hub job; order book at RM2.93b

    GDB Holdings won a contract to demolish a factory and build a two-block, five-storey logistics hub in Section 15, Shah Alam, due by September 2029. With an earlier RM1.02 billion ParkCity job, its outstanding order book rises to RM2.93 billion.

    GDBSource: The Edge ↗
  6. Energy

    Tenaga and IOI Corp team up on a 500MW solar project in Bahau

    Tenaga's TNB Renewables (70%), an IOI Corp unit (25%) and a consortium of 12 SMEs (5%) will develop a 500MW solar project with 250MW/1,000MWh of battery storage in Bahau, Negeri Sembilan, with maximum combined equity of RM606.2 million.

    TENAGAIOICORPSource: The Edge ↗
  7. Corporate

    IOIPG plans Singapore private property fund; 50% stake sale could raise nearly RM10b

    IOI Properties plans a private real estate fund backed by S$6 billion (RM19.1 billion) of Singapore assets; selling a 50% stake could raise close to RM10 billion. It targets a secondary listing on the Singapore Exchange in early 2027 and aims to cut net gearing from about 85% to 30%–50% over three to five years.

    IOIPGSource: The Edge ↗
  8. Expansion

    Paramount and Mitsui Fudosan partner on RM1.34b serviced apartment project

    Paramount Corp (51%) and Japan's Mitsui Fudosan (49%) will jointly develop The Ashbourne, a 458-unit serviced apartment project worth RM1.34 billion, with construction from the fourth quarter of 2026 and completion by end-2030.

    PARAMONSource: The Edge ↗
  9. Contract

    Sapura Industrial wins about RM307m Chery axle deal; Binastra lands RM303.8m automated warehouse job

    Sapura Industrial signed an axle assembly contract with Chery worth about RM307 million over 4.5 years, its first direct deal with the carmaker. Binastra won a RM303.8 million automated warehouse contract from PTT Synergy, with works running from 11 November 2026 to 30 September 2027.

    SAPINDBNASTRASource: The Edge ↗
  10. Corporate

    Public Bank sets up RM20b Islamic medium-term note programme

    Public Bank established a RM20 billion Islamic medium-term note programme with a 30-year tenure, with preliminary ratings from RAM of AAA for senior and AA1 for subordinated notes.

    PBBANKSource: The Edge ↗
