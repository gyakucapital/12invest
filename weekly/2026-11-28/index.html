12Invest Weekly Wrap · WEEKLY

KLCI gains 32.73 points this week to 1,589.24, ending a two-week slide; results season peaks as Petronas Chemicals and Genting disappoint

Sat, 28 Nov 202612Invest
FBM KLCI · this week（11 月 23 日–11 月 27 日）
1,589.24
Change
+32.73（+2.10%）

The KLCI fell on Monday and then rose for four straight sessions to close Friday at 1,589.24, up 32.73 points from the previous Friday's 1,556.51 and ending two straight weekly losses. A strong early-week rebound on Wall Street lifted regional sentiment and Friday saw buying in industrial products and utilities heavyweights, although several big companies reported weak results and the ringgit briefly fell to a near five-month low. Source: Focus Malaysia（Bernama） ↗

  1. IPO

    IPO round-up: Impact Capital opens 45% higher and ends slightly up; IOIPG REIT closes just below offer price, Yes Group flat

    Impact Capital debuted on the ACE Market on Monday at 32 sen, 45.5% above its 22 sen IPO price, and closed at 22.5 sen, still 2.3% higher. IOIPG Malaysia REIT opened on the Main Market on Wednesday at 87 sen and closed at 90.5 sen, just under its 91 sen final price, while Yes Group opened at 18.5 sen on Thursday and closed at its 18 sen IPO price. Emits' public portion was oversubscribed 6.43 times ahead of its 1 December listing; SCA Solutions closed applications on Thursday with balloting on 30 November; EMPG (28 sen) and KK Mart (up to 55 sen) opened their offers this week, both closing on 2 December.

    IMPACTIOIPGREITYESGROUPEMITSSCAEMPGKKMARTSource: Dagang News ↗
  2. Global

    Jensen Huang returns to lead Nvidia; US stocks rebound on Monday with the Nasdaq up 6.51%

    After last Friday's heavy losses, US stocks rebounded across the board on Monday (23 November), with the Nasdaq up 6.51% and the Dow up 3.01%, after Nvidia's board reappointed Jensen Huang as president and CEO and its shares jumped more than 31%. Regional sentiment improved and the KLCI snapped a five-day losing streak on Tuesday led by tech stocks, though chip stocks slipped again mid-week.

    Source: Fox Business ↗
  3. Market

    Ringgit weakens to 4.1220 against the US dollar, its weakest since end-June

    The ringgit weakened to 4.1220 against the US dollar early in the week, its lowest level since end-June 2026, and external uncertainty kept trading on Bursa cautious. With results season under way, traders said individual stocks would move mainly on their earnings.

    Source: The Star ↗
  4. Results

    Maybank's third-quarter net profit rises to RM2.74 billion

    Maybank's third-quarter net profit rose to RM2.74 billion from RM2.62 billion a year earlier, with net operating income up 0.5% to RM7.5 billion. Nine-month net profit was RM7.92 billion, against RM7.84 billion a year earlier.

    MAYBANKSource: The Star ↗
  5. Results

    Petronas Chemicals swings to RM179 million third-quarter profit but draws broker downgrades

    Petronas Chemicals posted a third-quarter net profit of RM179 million against a RM289 million net loss a year earlier, with revenue rising to RM7.62 billion as a bigger contribution from Pengerang lifted volumes. Its core profit fell sharply, however, and at least four brokers cut their ratings, with analysts worried that global oversupply and weak demand will keep weighing on earnings.

    PCHEMSource: The Star ↗
  6. Results

    Genting slips to RM161.4 million third-quarter loss; Genting Malaysia's profit falls 66%

    Genting's third-quarter revenue rose 7.3% to RM8.02 billion, but it posted a net loss of RM161.4 million against a RM30.3 million profit a year earlier. Genting Malaysia's revenue grew 29.1% to RM4.34 billion while its net profit fell 66.1% to RM40.6 million, and the group is cautious on the near-term outlook.

    GENTINGGENMSource: The Star ↗
  7. Results

    Hengyuan's third-quarter net profit jumps to RM557.95 million

    Hengyuan earned RM557.95 million in the third quarter, up from just RM21.04 million a year earlier, as revenue grew 51.4% to RM5.48 billion. Nine-month net profit was RM1.68 billion against a RM332.65 million loss a year earlier; the refiner cited higher product prices and expects fuel supply to stay tight.

    HENGYUANSource: The Star（Bernama） ↗
  8. Results

    TM's third-quarter net profit falls 28% to RM494 million; 10 sen dividend declared

    TM's third-quarter net profit fell to RM494 million from RM686.3 million a year earlier, mainly because the earlier quarter included a one-off fair value gain on investments, while revenue edged up 0.3% to RM3 billion. It declared a 10 sen interim dividend and lowered its capex guidance to 16% to 18% of revenue.

    TMSource: The Star ↗
  9. Results

    Sunway's construction order book hits a record RM12.45 billion; third-quarter revenue up 18%

    Sunway's third-quarter revenue rose 18% to RM3.03 billion, while net profit eased to RM314.06 million from RM338.14 million. Its construction arm has won RM9.8 billion of new jobs so far this year, above its full-year target, taking the outstanding order book to a record RM12.45 billion.

    SUNWAYSource: The Star ↗
  10. Results

    AirAsia Group posts RM175.11 million third-quarter net loss

    AirAsia Group posted a third-quarter net loss of RM175.11 million on revenue of RM4.66 billion, taking its nine-month net loss to RM857.16 million. It blamed jet fuel prices pushed up by the West Asia conflict and weaker regional currencies against the US dollar.

    AAGBSource: The Star ↗
