周一屠妖节补假后，马股连跌四个交易日，周三跌破 1,600 点关口，周五收 1,582.59 点，较上周五的 1,616.58 点下跌 33.99 点。辉达领导层风波引发 AI 相关交易修正，加上美国通胀高于预期推高美债收益率，银行及数据中心相关权值股遭抛售，投资者情绪持续谨慎。 来源: Free Malaysia Today (Bernama) ↗

    本周新股：SKA Capital 首日收涨 15%，Impact Capital 获 139.69 倍超购，IOIPG REIT 定价 91 仙

    SKA Capital 周三登陆 ACE 市场，以 51 仙开盘、收 49.5 仙，较 43 仙招股价高 15.1%。Impact Capital（22 仙）公众部分获 139.69 倍超额认购；Big Caring 最终定价每股 RM1.08，公众部分超购 4.31 倍；IOIPG Malaysia REIT 最终定价每单位 91 仙（低于 96 仙上限），公众部分认购 1.59 倍。Yes Group 申购周五截止、11 月 17 日抽签，Emits 申购至 11 月 19 日；KEB、MyDCD 和 Big Caring 将于下周（11 月 16、17 及 19 日）挂牌。

    SKACAPIMPACTBCGIOIPGREITYESGROUPEMITSKEBMYDCD来源： The Star ↗
  2. 环球

    黄仁勋请辞辉达 CEO，全球 AI 股受冲击

    辉达董事会上周日宣布调查黄仁勋与一名被指为中国间谍的女商人之关系，黄仁勋其后辞去 CEO 职务，该股自周日起市值蒸发约 3,000 亿美元。风波拖累日韩及欧洲科技股，AI 相关交易持续修正，也是本周马股情绪偏弱的主因之一。

    来源： The Edge Malaysia ↗
  3. 环球

    美国 10 月通胀高于预期，10 年期美债收益率升至 5%

    美国 10 月消费者物价指数按月涨 0.4%，核心物价涨 0.3%，均高于预期；10 年期美债收益率大涨 13.8 个基点至 5.00%，市场押注联储局 12 月升息。高利率预期周三拖累本地金融权值股，综指跌破 1,600 点。

    来源： TheStreet ↗

    Hartalega 次季净利增 79% 至 RM32.80 million

    HARTA来源： BernamaBiz ↗

    联合种植第三季净利升至 RM290.93 million，派息 45 仙

    UTDPLT来源： The Star ↗
  6. 业绩

    Heineken 大马第三季净利跌 39%，本季不派息

    HEIM来源： The Star ↗
  7. 合约

    SunCon 今年新订单达 RM8.06 billion，手头订单 RM11.7 billion

    SUNCON来源： The Star ↗
  8. 能源

    Dialog 追加 2.11 亿美元开发 Fatimah 气田

    Dialog 集团就 Fatimah 气田作出最终投资决定，额外投资 2.11 亿美元，资金来自内部资金及/或借贷。邻近的 Salbiah 气田已于 10 月 27 日投产，料贡献 2027 财年盈利；Fatimah 则预计贡献未来盈利。

    DIALOG来源： The Star ↗
  9. 企业动态

    Zetrix AI 债券违约、股息拖欠，交易所限期披露

    大马交易所指示 Zetrix AI 最迟 11 月 23 日派发拖欠的每股 2.89 仙终期股息，并给予延期至 11 月 13 日披露财务状况、独立董事及外部审计师辞职等事项。公司未能偿还一笔 RM25 million 伊斯兰债券，MARC 已将其 RM2 billion 债券计划评级降至违约级 D-IS；该股周三跌至 5.5 仙。

    ZETRIX来源： The Edge Malaysia ↗
  10. 大市

    9 月工业生产按年增 6.3%，制造业增速加快

    统计局数据显示，9 月工业生产指数按年增长 6.3%，其中制造业增 9.4%（8 月为 7.4%）、电力增 10.7%，矿业则下滑 9.4%。另外，大马棕油局数据显示 10 月底棕油库存按月增 3.15% 至 362 万吨。

12Invest Weekly Wrap · WEEKLY

KLCI falls 33.99 points this week to 1,582.59, a fifth straight daily loss; Jensen Huang's exit as Nvidia CEO hits AI stocks, SKA Capital closes debut 15% up

Sat, 14 Nov 202612Invest
FBM KLCI · this week（11 月 9 日–11 月 13 日）
1,582.59
Change
-33.99（-2.10%）

After the Deepavali holiday on Monday, Bursa fell on all four trading days, slipping below 1,600 on Wednesday and closing Friday at 1,582.59, down 33.99 points from the previous Friday's 1,616.58. Nvidia's leadership turmoil triggered a pullback in AI trades and hotter US inflation pushed up Treasury yields, prompting selling in banking and data-centre-related heavyweights as sentiment stayed cautious. Source: Free Malaysia Today (Bernama) ↗

  1. IPO

    IPO round-up: SKA Capital ends debut 15% up, Impact Capital 139.69 times oversubscribed, IOIPG REIT priced at 91 sen

    SKA Capital opened at 51 sen on its ACE Market debut on Wednesday and closed at 49.5 sen, 15.1% above its 43 sen IPO price. Impact Capital's public portion (22 sen) was oversubscribed 139.69 times; Big Caring set its final price at RM1.08 with the public portion oversubscribed 4.31 times; IOIPG Malaysia REIT fixed its final price at 91 sen a unit (below the 96 sen cap), with the public portion 1.59 times subscribed. Yes Group's offer closed on Friday with balloting on 17 November, Emits takes applications until 19 November, and KEB, MyDCD and Big Caring list next week (16, 17 and 19 November).

    SKACAPIMPACTBCGIOIPGREITYESGROUPEMITSKEBMYDCDSource: The Star ↗
  2. Global

    Jensen Huang quits as Nvidia CEO, rattling AI stocks worldwide

    Nvidia's board said last Sunday it was investigating Jensen Huang's ties to a businesswoman alleged to be a Chinese spy, and Huang then resigned as CEO; the stock has shed about US$300 billion in market value since Sunday. The turmoil weighed on tech stocks in Japan, South Korea and Europe, and the continued unwinding of AI trades was a key drag on Bursa sentiment this week.

    Source: The Edge Malaysia ↗
  3. Global

    Hotter US October inflation lifts the 10-year Treasury yield to 5%

    US consumer prices rose 0.4% month on month in October and core prices 0.3%, both above forecasts; the 10-year Treasury yield jumped 13.8 basis points to 5.00% as markets bet on a Fed hike in December. Higher-rate expectations hit local financial heavyweights on Wednesday, pushing the KLCI below 1,600.

    Source: TheStreet ↗
  4. Results

    Hartalega's second-quarter net profit rises 79% to RM32.80 million

    Hartalega's revenue for the second quarter of FY2027 (to 30 September) rose to RM644.74 million and net profit to RM32.80 million from RM18.29 million a year earlier, mainly on higher average selling prices. First-half net profit was RM102.83 million, and the company will gradually restart its plants, starting with Plant 3.

    HARTASource: BernamaBiz ↗
  5. Results

    United Plantations' third-quarter net profit rises to RM290.93 million; 45 sen in dividends

    United Plantations' third-quarter revenue was RM722.22 million (RM677.08 million a year earlier) and net profit rose to RM290.93 million from RM203.28 million, on higher output, better crude palm oil prices and a bigger refinery contribution. It declared an interim dividend of 30 sen and a special dividend of 15 sen, payable on 8 December.

    UTDPLTSource: The Star ↗
  6. Results

    Heineken Malaysia's third-quarter net profit falls 39%; no dividend this quarter

    Heineken Malaysia's third-quarter revenue fell 18% year on year to RM539.1 million and net profit 39% to RM69.2 million, while nine-month net profit dropped 30% to RM224.2 million. It blamed weak consumer sentiment, an extra 10% excise duty and distributor destocking, and expects inventory to normalise in the fourth quarter.

    HEIMSource: The Star ↗
  7. Contracts

    SunCon's new orders this year reach RM8.06 billion; order book at RM11.7 billion

    After last week's RM920.8 million MEP fit-out job at a Johor data centre for an existing US multinational client, Sunway Construction's new orders so far this year total RM8.06 billion, putting it on track for the upper end of its RM7 billion to RM9 billion target; its order book stood at about RM11.7 billion as of November.

    SUNCONSource: The Star ↗
  8. Energy

    Dialog commits a further US$211 million to develop the Fatimah gas field

    Dialog Group has taken the final investment decision on the Fatimah gas field, investing an additional US$211 million funded by internal funds and/or borrowings. The neighbouring Salbiah field started production on 27 October and is expected to contribute to FY2027 earnings, while Fatimah should add to earnings in later years.

    DIALOGSource: The Star ↗
  9. Corporate

    Zetrix AI hit by sukuk default and unpaid dividend as Bursa sets deadlines

    Bursa Malaysia directed Zetrix AI to pay its overdue final dividend of 2.89 sen a share by 23 November and gave it until 13 November to disclose its financial position and the resignations of its independent directors and external auditor. The company failed to repay a RM25 million sukuk, and MARC cut its RM2 billion sukuk programme to the default grade D-IS; the stock fell to 5.5 sen on Wednesday.

    ZETRIXSource: The Edge Malaysia ↗
  10. Market

    September industrial output up 6.3% year on year as manufacturing picks up

    Statistics department data showed the industrial production index rose 6.3% year on year in September, with manufacturing up 9.4% (7.4% in August) and electricity up 10.7%, while mining fell 9.4%. Separately, MPOB data showed palm oil stocks rose 3.15% month on month to 3.62 million tonnes at end-October.

    Source: The Star ↗
