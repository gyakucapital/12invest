综指周一逢低反弹，随后连跌四个交易日，周五收 1,556.51 点，较上周五的 1,582.59 点下跌 26.08 点，连续第二周走低。美股科技股回落及美国收紧先进芯片出口拖累数据中心相关权值股，周四银行股买盘仅收复部分失地，市场情绪偏向谨慎。 来源: Free Malaysia Today（Bernama） ↗

  1. IPO

    本周新股：KEB、MyDCD 首日跌近两成，Big Caring 主板首日微涨 0.9%

    KEB 周一登陆 ACE 市场，以 35 仙招股价开盘、收 28.5 仙，跌 18.6%；MyDCD 周二以 48 仙开盘、收 45 仙，较 56 仙招股价低 19.6%；Big Caring 周四在主板以 RM1.05 开盘，其后回升至 RM1.09 收市，较 RM1.08 招股价高 0.9%。Yes Group 公众部分获 2.8 倍超额认购，Emits 申购周四截止、11 月 23 日抽签，SCA Solutions（28 仙）周三起接受申购至 11 月 26 日；下周 Impact Capital、IOIPG Malaysia REIT 及 Yes Group 将于 11 月 24 至 26 日先后挂牌。

    KEBMYDCDBCGYESGROUPEMITSSCAIMPACTIOIPGREIT来源： The Star ↗
  2. 大市

    大马第三季经济增长 5.5%，首九个月增 5.6%

    国家银行公布，第三季国内生产总值按年增长 5.5%，科技相关出口持续走强，私人投资增 9.4%、私人消费增 4.3%；制造业增 7.7%，服务业增 5.8%，矿业则萎缩 6.1%。首九个月经济增长 5.6%，国行预计全年增长超过 5%，2027 年介于 4.2% 至 5.2%。

    来源： Malay Mail（Bernama） ↗
  3. 政策

    美国先进芯片输往大马须申请准证，11 月 19 日起生效

    美国商务部发布临时最终规则，向马来西亚、新加坡、印尼、泰国及巴西出口或转运先进半导体须先取得准证，数据中心、晶圆代工厂及芯片出口商须对客户进行尽职调查，美国总部企业在符合申报要求下获豁免。新规公布后，本地数据中心相关股本周持续受压。

    来源： The Edge Malaysia ↗
  4. 环球

    辉达第三季营收按年增 103% 至 1,158 亿美元

    辉达截至 10 月 25 日的 2027 财年第三季营收达 1,158 亿美元，按年增 103%，其中数据中心业务贡献 1,076 亿美元；公司预计第四季营收约 1,300 亿美元，且未计入中国数据中心算力收入。这是黄仁勋卸任 CEO 后的首份季报，不过美股科技股本周仍起伏不定。

    来源： NVIDIA ↗

12Invest Weekly Wrap · WEEKLY

KLCI falls 26.08 points this week to 1,556.51; US chip export rule hits data-centre stocks, Malaysia's economy grows 5.5% in the third quarter

Sat, 21 Nov 202612Invest
FBM KLCI · this week（11 月 16 日–11 月 20 日）
1,556.51
Change
-26.08（-1.65%）

The KLCI rebounded on bargain hunting on Monday, then fell for four straight sessions to close Friday at 1,556.51, down 26.08 points from the previous Friday's 1,582.59 and a second straight weekly loss. Weaker US tech stocks and tighter US curbs on advanced chip exports weighed on data-centre-related heavyweights, and Thursday's buying in banks recovered only part of the losses as sentiment stayed cautious. Source: Free Malaysia Today（Bernama） ↗

  1. IPO

    IPO round-up: KEB and MyDCD fall almost 20% on debut, Big Caring ends Main Market debut 0.9% up

    KEB opened at its 35 sen IPO price on its ACE Market debut on Monday and closed at 28.5 sen, down 18.6%; MyDCD opened at 48 sen on Tuesday and closed at 45 sen, 19.6% below its 56 sen IPO price; Big Caring opened at RM1.05 on the Main Market on Thursday and recovered to close at RM1.09, 0.9% above its RM1.08 IPO price. Yes Group's public portion was oversubscribed 2.8 times, Emits closed applications on Thursday with balloting on 23 November, and SCA Solutions (28 sen) opened its offer on Wednesday until 26 November; Impact Capital, IOIPG Malaysia REIT and Yes Group list next week from 24 to 26 November.

    KEBMYDCDBCGYESGROUPEMITSSCAIMPACTIOIPGREITSource: The Star ↗
  2. Market

    Malaysia's economy grows 5.5% in the third quarter, 5.6% over nine months

    Bank Negara said GDP grew 5.5% year on year in the third quarter as tech-related exports stayed strong, with private investment up 9.4% and private consumption up 4.3%; manufacturing rose 7.7% and services 5.8%, while mining shrank 6.1%. Growth for the first nine months was 5.6%, and the central bank expects full-year growth above 5% and 4.2% to 5.2% in 2027.

    Source: Malay Mail（Bernama） ↗
  3. Policy

    US licence rule for advanced chips to Malaysia takes effect on 19 November

    The US Commerce Department issued an interim final rule requiring a licence to export or re-export advanced semiconductors to Malaysia, Singapore, Indonesia, Thailand and Brazil, with data centres, foundries and chip exporters required to carry out customer due diligence; US-headquartered companies are exempt if they meet reporting requirements. Local data-centre-related stocks stayed under pressure through the week after the rule was announced.

    Source: The Edge Malaysia ↗
  4. Global

    Nvidia's third-quarter revenue more than doubles to US$115.8 billion

    Nvidia's revenue for the third quarter of FY2027 (to 25 October) rose 103% year on year to US$115.8 billion, with US$107.6 billion from data centres; it guided for fourth-quarter revenue of about US$130 billion, excluding any data-centre compute revenue from China. It was the first report since Jensen Huang stepped down as CEO, though US tech stocks stayed volatile this week.

    Source: NVIDIA ↗
  5. Contracts

    Gamuda wins Keelung MRT job in Taiwan worth RM2.61 billion

    A joint venture of Gamuda and Taiwan's Feng Shun Construction won part of the design-and-build works for the Keelung underground MRT from New Taipei City's Department of Rapid Transit Systems, worth NT$21.02 billion (about RM2.61 billion) over 2,700 days; Gamuda holds 80%, a share of about RM2.09 billion.

    GAMUDASource: theSun ↗
  6. Contracts

    TM appointed for SALAM submarine cable project, capped at RM2.7 billion

    Telekom Malaysia's TM Technology Services was appointed universal service provider for Part 1 of the MADANI submarine cable link (SALAM), covering the design, laying and commissioning of four submarine cable segments at an approved cost of up to RM2.7 billion. Work is set to start on 15 December and be completed in phases by the first quarter of 2030.

    TMSource: The Star ↗
  7. Results

    CelcomDigi's third-quarter net profit rises 20% to RM408 million; 3.4 sen dividend

    CelcomDigi's third-quarter net profit rose to RM408 million from RM341 million a year earlier and revenue grew 5.8% to RM3.31 billion, driven by postpaid, home fibre and enterprise. It declared an interim dividend of 3.4 sen a share, bringing dividends for the year to 10.2 sen.

    CDBSource: The Edge Malaysia ↗
  8. Results

    Kossan's third-quarter net profit jumps 131% to RM87.41 million

    Kossan's third-quarter net profit rose to RM87.41 million from RM37.83 million a year earlier and revenue grew 53% to RM671.12 million on higher average selling prices and volumes, partly offset by forex effects from a stronger ringgit; it declared an interim dividend of 2.5 sen a share.

    KOSSANSource: The Edge Malaysia ↗
  9. Results

    Inari's first-quarter net profit rises 18.5% to RM61 million

    Inari's net profit for the first quarter of FY2027 (to 30 September) rose 18.5% year on year to RM61 million and revenue 13.1% to RM368.5 million, as volumes rose across all segments; it proposed dividends totalling 1.5 sen and remains optimistic about the rest of the financial year.

    INARISource: The Star ↗
  10. Corporate

    Zetrix AI says it cannot repay its RM2 billion sukuk

    Zetrix AI told Bursa it is currently unable to repay all amounts outstanding under its RM2 billion sukuk programme and is negotiating revised terms with sukuk holders, and it withdrew its first interim dividend for 2026; it expects the suspension of its Road Transport Department collection agency status to cut group revenue by about 15%.

    ZETRIXSource: The Edge Malaysia ↗
