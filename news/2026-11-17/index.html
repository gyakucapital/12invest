    来源： The Star (Bernama) ↗

12Invest Daily Bursa Brief · DAILY

KLCI falls 12.88 to 1,575.15; US tech sell-off, Gamuda wins RM2.61b Taiwan MRT job as MyDCD debuts today

Tue, 17 Nov 202612Invest
FBM KLCI · Tue, 17 Nov 2026 close
1,575.15
Change
-12.88（-0.81%）

Selling intensified in technology and data centre-related stocks after Wall Street's overnight losses, with the index trading between 1,570.22 and 1,585.10 before closing below 1,580; breadth was clearly negative, while volume rose from Monday. Source: Free Malaysia Today (Bernama) ↗

  1. Global

    Wall Street slumps on Monday as Nasdaq drops 2.49%; Nvidia reports tonight

    The Dow fell 739.38 points (-1.41%) to 51,608.17, the S&P 500 lost 1.92% to 7,416.59 and the Nasdaq slid 2.49% to 24,509.76. Nvidia fell 4.12% after a report that its board is not considering bringing back Jensen Huang; Nvidia's third-quarter results are due Tuesday, and Anthropic is expected to list on Thursday.

    Source: Yahoo Finance ↗
  2. Contract

    Gamuda wins RM2.61 billion Keelung underground MRT contract in Taiwan

    Gamuda's joint venture with Taiwan's Feng Shun Construction was awarded part of the design and construction of the Keelung Underground MRT by New Taipei City's Department of Rapid Transit Systems, worth NT$21.02 billion (about RM2.61 billion) over 2,700 days. Gamuda holds 80% of the JV, giving it a share of about RM2.09 billion.

    GAMUDASource: theSun ↗
  3. IPO

    MyDCD debuts on the ACE Market today after 36.46 times public oversubscription

    MyDCD, priced at 56 sen a share, lists on the ACE Market today. Its 89.7 million-share public portion drew 28,816 applications for 3.36 billion shares, an oversubscription of 36.46 times; the bumiputera portion was 24 times oversubscribed and the other public portion 48.92 times.

    MYDCDSource: The Star ↗
  4. Corporate

    NCT Alliance plans placement to raise up to RM152.47 million

    NCT Alliance proposes to place out up to 304.94 million new shares to independent third-party investors at an indicative 50 sen each, raising up to RM152.47 million. About 70% of the proceeds will go to two industrial parks in Selangor and Kedah with a combined remaining GDV of RM3.82 billion; completion is expected in the first half of 2027.

    NCTSource: The Star ↗
  5. Market

    Foreigners net sell RM270.2 million last week as retail buying extends to 12 weeks

    MBSB Investment Bank's fund flow report shows foreign investors net sold RM270.2 million in the week to Nov 13, ending two weeks of net inflows; construction drew the largest foreign inflow at RM123 million, while technology saw RM241.5 million of net selling. Local institutions net sold RM56 million and retailers net bought RM326.3 million.

    Source: The Star (Bernama) ↗
  6. Policy

    MBSB IB: proposed US curbs on remote AI chip access have limited impact on Malaysia's wider data centre sector

    MBSB Investment Bank says the proposed US restrictions on remote access to advanced AI chips are not a blanket ban on US AI chips in Malaysia, and that conventional cloud, colocation and enterprise computing should remain relatively resilient, with operators heavily exposed to Chinese customers or offering GPU-as-a-service most at risk. It found no credible evidence linking YTL Power to the alleged chip diversion, describing its business as mainly colocation.

    YTLPOWRSource: The Star ↗
  7. Energy

    Brent hovers near US$95 after fresh Hormuz tanker incidents

    As of 0757 GMT on Monday, Brent was up 5 cents at US$95.89 a barrel and WTI up 9 cents at US$88.65. Iran's Revolutionary Guards said two tankers struck mines, adding to Middle East supply worries, though the IEA's forecast of a fourth-quarter supply surplus capped gains.

    Source: The Star (Reuters) ↗
