马股涨 18.26 点收 1,589.24 点；云顶第三季转亏，国油化学核心盈利挫 83%

2026年11月27日（五）12Invest
FBM KLCI · 11月27日收市
1,589.24
涨跌
+18.26（+1.16%）
涨跌家数
涨 727 / 跌 363 / 平 474
成交
31.7 亿股，RM2.64 billion

综指周五跟随区域股市走高，盘中在 1,564.52 至 1,592.45 点之间波动，终场涨 18.26 点，工业产品与服务及公用事业领域部分重量股获买盘。涨股明显多于跌股，成交额则降至 RM2.64 billion。 来源: Focus Malaysia（Bernama） ↗

  1. 业绩

    云顶第三季营收增 7.3%，但转为净亏 RM161.4 million

    云顶（Genting）截至 9 月 30 日第三季营收增 7.3% 至 RM8.02 billion，但录得净亏 RM161.4 million，去年同期净赚 RM30.3 million。旗下云顶大马（Genting Malaysia）同季营收增 29.1% 至 RM4.34 billion，净利则跌 66.1% 至 RM40.6 million，公司对短期前景保持谨慎。

    GENTINGGENM来源： The Star ↗
  2. 业绩

    国油化学第三季核心盈利挫 83%，遭至少四家券商降级

    国油化学（PChem）第三季核心盈利大跌 83% 至 RM99 million，单日内至少四家券商下调其评级。分析员警告，全球供应过剩及需求疲弱或在未来两年继续拖累盈利。

    PCHEM来源： The Star ↗
  3. 业绩

    大马银行上半年净利 RM1.06 billion，派息 15 仙

    大马银行（AMMB）2027 财年上半年净利 RM1.06 billion，略高于去年同期的 RM1.05 billion；净利息收入按年增 5.9% 至 RM1.96 billion。公司宣布派发 15 仙中期股息，12 月 10 日除权，12 月 24 日派发。

    AMBANK来源： The Star ↗
  4. 业绩

    森那美首季净利增至 RM416 million，营收下滑

    森那美（Sime Darby）首季净利 RM416 million，高于去年同期的 RM355 million；营收则由 RM18.03 billion 降至 RM16.7 billion。旗下 UMW 税前盈利增 65.5% 至 RM432 million，汽车业务因中国市场竞争激烈而走低。

    SIME来源： The Star ↗
  5. 业绩

    IOI 产业首季营收增 16%，未入账销售 RM2.46 billion

    IOI 产业（IOI Properties）截至 9 月 30 日首季营收增 16.19% 至 RM1.1 billion，盈利按年跌 8.25% 至 RM609.5 million，主要因去年同期有 RM502.8 million 的重估收益。未入账销售达 RM2.46 billion，接近纪录水平，公司对前景保持乐观。

    IOIPG来源： The Star ↗
  6. 业绩

    大马回教保险第三季净利增 10.8% 至 RM127.9 million

    大马回教保险（Takaful Malaysia）第三季净利由 RM115.4 million 增至 RM127.9 million，营收增 25.2% 至 RM1.32 billion。首九个月净利 RM317.7 million，按年增 8.1%。

    TAKAFUL来源： The Star ↗
  7. 扩张

    Lianson Fleet 斥 RM65.88 million 购散货船，第三季净利增至 RM32.88 million

    Lianson Fleet（LFG）向无关第三方以 RM65.88 million 收购一艘超灵便型散货船，以扩展海运业务、降低对海事支援船的依赖。公司第三季净利 RM32.88 million，去年同期 RM24.24 million；营收 RM91.61 million，并派发 1 仙中期股息。

    LFG来源： The Star ↗

KLCI up 18.26 points at 1,589.24; Genting swings to 3Q loss, PChem core profit plunges 83%

Fri, 27 Nov 202612Invest
FBM KLCI · Fri, 27 Nov 2026 close
1,589.24
Change
+18.26（+1.16%）

The KLCI tracked upbeat regional markets on Friday, moving between 1,564.52 and 1,592.45 before closing 18.26 points higher on buying in selected industrial products and services and utilities heavyweights. Gainers clearly outnumbered losers, while turnover eased to RM2.64 billion. Source: Focus Malaysia（Bernama） ↗

  1. Results

    Genting 3Q revenue up 7.3% but swings to RM161.4 million net loss

    Genting's third-quarter revenue rose 7.3% to RM8.02 billion, but it posted a net loss of RM161.4 million against a net profit of RM30.3 million a year earlier. Unit Genting Malaysia grew revenue 29.1% to RM4.34 billion while net profit fell 66.1% to RM40.6 million; it remains cautious on near-term prospects.

    GENTINGGENMSource: The Star ↗
  2. Results

    PChem 3Q core profit plunges 83%, hit by at least four downgrades

    Petronas Chemicals' third-quarter core profit fell 83% to RM99 million, and at least four brokerages downgraded the stock in one day. Analysts warn global oversupply and weak demand could weigh on earnings for the next two years.

    PCHEMSource: The Star ↗
  3. Results

    AmBank 1H net profit at RM1.06 billion, 15 sen dividend

    AMMB Holdings posted 1HFY27 net profit of RM1.06 billion, slightly above RM1.05 billion a year earlier, with net interest income up 5.9% to RM1.96 billion. It declared a 15 sen interim dividend, going ex on Dec 10 and payable Dec 24.

    AMBANKSource: The Star ↗
  4. Results

    Sime Darby 1Q net profit rises to RM416 million on lower revenue

    Sime Darby's first-quarter net profit rose to RM416 million from RM355 million, while revenue fell to RM16.7 billion from RM18.03 billion. UMW's pre-tax profit jumped 65.5% to RM432 million; the motors division was weaker on intense competition in China.

    SIMESource: The Star ↗
  5. Results

    IOI Properties 1Q revenue up 16%, unbilled sales at RM2.46 billion

    IOI Properties' first-quarter revenue rose 16.19% to RM1.1 billion, while earnings fell 8.25% to RM609.5 million as the year-ago quarter included a RM502.8 million remeasurement gain. Unbilled sales stood near record levels at RM2.46 billion, and management says the outlook remains positive.

    IOIPGSource: The Star ↗
  6. Results

    Takaful Malaysia 3Q net profit up 10.8% to RM127.9 million

    Takaful Malaysia's third-quarter net profit rose to RM127.9 million from RM115.4 million, with revenue up 25.2% to RM1.32 billion. Nine-month net profit was RM317.7 million, up 8.1% year on year.

    TAKAFULSource: The Star ↗
  7. Expansion

    Lianson Fleet buys bulk carrier for RM65.88 million; 3Q profit rises to RM32.88 million

    Lianson Fleet is buying a Supramax bulk carrier from an unrelated party for RM65.88 million to expand its marine transport business beyond offshore support vessels. Its third-quarter net profit rose to RM32.88 million from RM24.24 million on revenue of RM91.61 million, and it declared a 1 sen interim dividend.

    LFGSource: The Star ↗
