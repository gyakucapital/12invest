  1. 环球

    黄仁勋请辞辉达 CEO，美股周四小幅收低

    辉达 CEO 黄仁勋辞职后，该股盘中一度重挫约 14%，其后收复约一半跌幅，其他 AI 芯片股则走高。标普 500 跌 0.4% 至 7,592.40 点，道指跌 189.40 点至 52,297.09 点，纳指跌 100.10 点至 25,366.09 点；特朗普表示不再有意与伊朗达成协议，油价走高。

    来源： KRMG（AP） ↗
  2. 环球

    辉达风波拖累全球 AI 股，日韩股市收低约 0.7%

    辉达董事会周日宣布调查黄仁勋与一名被指为中国间谍的女商人之关系，该股自周日以来市值蒸发约 3,000 亿美元，盘前一度跌 7.8%。日本及韩国股市周四收低近 0.7%，欧洲科技股跌约 1%；CoreWeave、Nebius、美光等 AI 相关股走低，AMD 与博通则逆市上涨。

    来源： The Edge Malaysia ↗
  3. 业绩

    Hartalega 次季净利增 79% 至 RM32.80 million

    Hartalega 截至 9 月 30 日的 2027 财年次季营收增至 RM644.74 million（上财年同期 RM539.66 million），净利由 RM18.29 million 升至 RM32.80 million，主要受平均售价上扬带动，销量大致持平。首半年净利 RM102.83 million；公司将逐步重启生产厂房，先从 3 号厂开始。

    HARTA来源： BernamaBiz ↗
  4. 业绩

    柔佛种植第三季净利跌至 RM100.26 million

    柔佛种植集团第三季营收 RM450.71 million，低于上年同期的 RM496.15 million；净利由 RM106.03 million 降至 RM100.26 million。原棕油交付量减 15.9% 至 78,451 公吨，自产原棕油成本则增 13.5% 至每公吨 RM2,526；公司提醒 11 月开始的季候风或影响作业。

    JPG来源： BernamaBiz ↗
  5. 业绩

    Heineken 大马第三季净利跌 39%，未派息

    Heineken 大马第三季营收按年跌 18% 至 RM539.1 million，净利跌 39% 至 RM69.2 million；首九个月净利 RM224.2 million，减 30%。公司归咎于消费情绪低迷、去年 11 月起额外 10% 消费税及经销商库存调整，预计第四季完成库存正常化，本季不派息。

    HEIM来源： The Star ↗

    Zetrix AI 今天须向交易所披露财务状况

    大马交易所给予 Zetrix AI 的延长期限今天（11 月 13 日）届满，公司须披露财务状况、股息未派问题、独立董事及外部审计师辞职，以及子公司被暂停陆路交通局代收资格的影响。该股周三收 5.5 仙。

    ZETRIX来源： The Edge Malaysia ↗

KLCI falls for a fifth day to 1,582.59; Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang resigns as US indices slip 0.4%

Fri, 13 Nov 202612Invest
FBM KLCI · Fri, 13 Nov 2026 close
1,582.59
Change
-3.37（-0.21%）

A lack of buying interest and persistently cautious sentiment sent the index lower for a fifth straight session, touching an intraday low of 1,579.66; the ongoing correction in AI-related trades and uncertainty over Nvidia's leadership continued to weigh on the market. Source: Free Malaysia Today (Bernama) ↗

  1. Global

    Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang resigns; Wall Street ends slightly lower on Thursday

    Nvidia fell as much as about 14% after Huang resigned before recovering about half the drop, while other AI chip stocks rose. The S&P 500 slipped 0.4% to 7,592.40, the Dow fell 189.40 points to 52,297.09 and the Nasdaq lost 100.10 points to 25,366.09. Oil climbed after President Trump said he was no longer interested in a deal with Iran.

    Source: KRMG（AP） ↗
  2. Global

    Nvidia turmoil drags global AI stocks; Japan and South Korea close about 0.7% lower

    Nvidia's board on Sunday opened a probe into Huang's relationship with a businesswoman alleged to be a Chinese spy; the stock has lost about US$300 billion in value since then and fell 7.8% pre-market. Japan and South Korea closed nearly 0.7% lower and European tech fell about 1%; AI names such as CoreWeave, Nebius and Micron slid, while AMD and Broadcom rose.

    Source: The Edge Malaysia ↗
  3. Results

    Hartalega's 2Q net profit rises 79% to RM32.80 million

    For 2QFY27 ended Sept 30, Hartalega's revenue rose to RM644.74 million (RM539.66 million a year earlier) and net profit to RM32.80 million from RM18.29 million, driven by higher average selling prices on broadly stable volumes. First-half net profit was RM102.83 million; the group will progressively recommission plants, starting with Plant 3.

    HARTASource: BernamaBiz ↗
  4. Results

    Johor Plantations' 3Q net profit eases to RM100.26 million

    Johor Plantations Group's 3Q revenue was RM450.71 million versus RM496.15 million a year earlier, and net profit fell to RM100.26 million from RM106.03 million. CPO deliveries dropped 15.9% to 78,451 tonnes while internal CPO production cost rose 13.5% to RM2,526 a tonne; the group flagged possible monsoon disruptions from November.

    JPGSource: BernamaBiz ↗
  5. Results

    Heineken Malaysia's 3Q net profit falls 39%, no dividend

    Heineken Malaysia's 3Q revenue fell 18% year on year to RM539.1 million and net profit 39% to RM69.2 million; nine-month net profit was RM224.2 million, down 30%. It cited weak consumer sentiment, the extra 10% excise duty since Nov 2025 and distributor inventory normalisation, which it expects to conclude in 4Q. No dividend was declared for the quarter.

    HEIMSource: The Star ↗
  6. Corporate

    Zetrix AI's deadline to disclose its financial position falls today

    Bursa Malaysia's extended deadline for Zetrix AI expires today (Nov 13): the company must disclose its financial position, the unpaid dividend, the resignation of its independent directors and external auditors, and the impact of its unit's suspension as a JPJ collection agent. The stock closed at 5.5 sen on Wednesday.

    ZETRIXSource: The Edge Malaysia ↗
  7. IPO

    Three IPOs list next week: KEB, MyDCD and Big Caring

    Under their prospectus timetables, KEB lists on Monday (Nov 16), MyDCD on Nov 17 and pharmacy chain Big Caring on the Main Market on Nov 19; Big Caring's public portion was 4.31 times oversubscribed and its final price set at RM1.08 a share.

    KEBMYDCDBCGSource: 12Invest IPO 日历 ↗
