  1. 环球

    美股隔夜强劲反弹：纳指飙 6.51%，辉达涨逾 31%

    美股周一（11 月 23 日）全面反弹，道指涨 1,447.98 点或 3.01% 至 49,595.31 点，标普 500 指数涨 4.73%，纳斯达克指数大涨 6.51%，摆脱上周跌入调整区的颓势。辉达董事部重新委任黄仁勋为总裁，股价飙升逾 31%，带动 AI 及数据中心股回升。

    来源： Fox Business ↗
  2. 业绩

    马银行第三季净利增至 RM2.74 billion

    马银行截至 9 月 30 日第三季净利为 RM2.74 billion，高于去年同期的 RM2.62 billion；营收则由 RM16.6 billion 降至 RM14.57 billion，净营运收入按年增 0.5% 至 RM7.5 billion。首九个月净利为 RM7.92 billion，去年同期为 RM7.84 billion。

    MAYBANK来源： The Star ↗
  3. 业绩

    亚航集团第三季净亏 RM175.11 million，燃油及汇率成拖累

    亚航集团（AirAsia Group）第三季净亏 RM175.11 million，营收 RM4.66 billion；首九个月净亏 RM857.16 million，营收 RM15.69 billion。集团指西亚冲突推高航空燃油价格，加上令吉、泰铢、印尼盾及菲律宾比索兑美元走软，冲击业绩；因反向收购，财报未提供去年同期比较数据。

    AAGB来源： The Star ↗

    Impact Capital 今日登陆 ACE 市场，IOIPG REIT 明日挂牌

    Impact Capital 今日（11 月 24 日）以每股 22 仙在 ACE 市场挂牌，公众部分获 139.69 倍超额认购；IOIPG Malaysia REIT 订于明日（11 月 25 日）在主板上市，Yes Group 则于 11 月 26 日以 18 仙登场。

    IMPACTIOIPGREITYESGROUP来源： rates.my ↗

    令吉兑美元跌至 4.1220，创 6 月底以来最弱

    来源： The Star ↗
  6. 大市

    本周焦点：美国第三季 GDP 及 10 月 PCE 数据

    市场本周关注周三公布的美国第三季 GDP 第二次预估及 10 月 PCE 通胀数据，以及美联储利率走向；美国周四感恩节休市，环球交投或转淡。

KLCI fell 7.46 points to 1,549.05; Wall Street rebounds as Nasdaq jumps 6.51%, Maybank 3Q profit rises

1,549.05
Change
-7.46（-0.47%）

The KLCI opened lower yesterday, moved between 1,544.96 and 1,555.25 and closed down 7.46 points. Selling in utilities heavyweights such as Tenaga Nasional, YTL Power and YTL Corp weighed on the market, while a weaker ringgit at 4.1220 per US dollar and a 3.31% drop in South Korea's Kospi hurt sentiment. Source: The Star ↗

  1. Global

    Wall Street rebounds overnight: Nasdaq up 6.51%, Nvidia jumps over 31%

    US stocks rallied on Monday (Nov 23). The Dow rose 1,447.98 points or 3.01% to 49,595.31, the S&P 500 gained 4.73% and the Nasdaq surged 6.51%, recovering from last week's slide into correction. Nvidia soared more than 31% after its board reinstated Jensen Huang as CEO, lifting AI and data-centre stocks.

    Source: Fox Business ↗
  2. Results

    Maybank 3Q net profit rises to RM2.74 billion

    Maybank's net profit for the third quarter ended Sept 30 rose to RM2.74 billion from RM2.62 billion a year earlier, while revenue fell to RM14.57 billion from RM16.6 billion; net operating income rose 0.5% to RM7.5 billion. Nine-month net profit was RM7.92 billion versus RM7.84 billion.

    MAYBANKSource: The Star ↗
  3. Results

    AirAsia Group posts RM175.11 million 3Q net loss on fuel and currency

    AirAsia Group posted a third-quarter net loss of RM175.11 million on revenue of RM4.66 billion, and a nine-month net loss of RM857.16 million on revenue of RM15.69 billion. It cited higher jet fuel prices due to the West Asia conflict and weaker regional currencies against the US dollar; no year-earlier comparison was given because of the reverse acquisition.

    AAGBSource: The Star ↗
  4. IPO

    Impact Capital debuts on ACE Market today, IOIPG REIT lists tomorrow

    Impact Capital lists on the ACE Market today (Nov 24) at 22 sen a share after its public portion was oversubscribed 139.69 times; IOIPG Malaysia REIT is set to list on the Main Market tomorrow (Nov 25), followed by Yes Group at 18 sen on Nov 26.

    IMPACTIOIPGREITYESGROUPSource: rates.my ↗
  5. Market

    Ringgit weakens to 4.1220 against the US dollar, lowest since late June

    The ringgit weakened to 4.1220 against the US dollar yesterday, its lowest since late June 2026, which together with external uncertainty kept trading on Bursa cautious. Dealers said company earnings will drive individual stock moves during the results season.

    Source: The Star ↗
  6. Market

    This week: US 3Q GDP and October PCE data in focus

    Markets are watching Wednesday's second estimate of US third-quarter GDP and October PCE inflation data, as well as the Fed rate outlook; the US Thanksgiving holiday on Thursday may thin global trading.

    Source: Bernama ↗
