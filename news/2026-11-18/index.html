  1. 环球

    辉达第三季营收按年增 103% 至 1,158 亿美元，本季指引 1,300 亿美元

    辉达截至 10 月 25 日的 2027 财年第三季营收达 1,158 亿美元，按季增 20%、按年增 103%，其中数据中心业务贡献 1,076 亿美元。公司预计第四季营收约 1,300 亿美元（上下 3%），并假设无来自中国的数据中心算力收入；这是黄仁勋卸任后的首份季报。

    来源： NVIDIA ↗
  2. 政策

    美国先进芯片出口大马须申请准证，11 月 19 日起生效

    美国商务部发布临时最终规则，向马来西亚、新加坡、印尼、泰国和巴西出口或转运先进半导体须先取得准证，数据中心、晶圆代工厂及芯片出口商须对客户进行尽职调查。总部设于美国的企业在符合申报要求下获豁免；规则同时正式撤销拜登时期的 AI 扩散规则。

    来源： The Edge ↗
  3. 合约

    大马电讯获批承建 SALAM 海底电缆，顶限 RM2.7 billion

    大马电讯旗下 TM Technology Services 获委为“MADANI 海底电缆连接”（SALAM）第一部分的普及服务供应商，负责四段海底电缆的设计、制造、铺设、测试与启用，批准成本最高 RM2.7 billion。工程预定 12 月 15 日动工，2030 年第一季前分阶段完成；公司仍在检讨相关条款。

    TM来源： The Star ↗

    戴乐首季净利增 18% 至 RM165.08 million

    DIALOG来源： The Star ↗

    来源： The Star ↗

KLCI falls 0.53% to 1,566.73; Nvidia revenue jumps 103% as new US AI-chip export rule takes effect tomorrow

Wed, 18 Nov 202612Invest
FBM KLCI · Wed, 18 Nov 2026 close
1,566.73
Change
-8.42（-0.53%）

Cautious sentiment kept weighing on financial and plantation heavyweights, pulling the index lower, though gains in technology counters capped the losses; the KLCI traded between 1,564.71 and 1,575.23, gainers outnumbered losers in the broader market, and turnover value rose from Tuesday. Source: Free Malaysia Today (Bernama) ↗

  1. Global

    Nvidia 3Q revenue up 103% to US$115.8 billion, guides US$130 billion for this quarter

    Nvidia's revenue for the third quarter of FY2027, ended Oct 25, reached US$115.8 billion, up 20% quarter-on-quarter and 103% year-on-year, with data centres contributing US$107.6 billion. It guides fourth-quarter revenue of about US$130 billion (plus or minus 3%), assuming no data-centre compute revenue from China. It is the first report since Jensen Huang stepped down.

    Source: NVIDIA ↗
  2. Policy

    US advanced-chip exports to Malaysia need a licence from Nov 19

    The US Commerce Department issued an interim final rule requiring a licence to export or transfer advanced semiconductors to Malaysia, Singapore, Indonesia, Thailand and Brazil, with data centres, foundries and chip exporters required to carry out customer due diligence. US-headquartered companies are exempt if they meet reporting requirements, and the rule formally rescinds the Biden-era AI diffusion rule.

    Source: The Edge ↗
  3. Contract

    TM approved to build SALAM submarine cable, capped at RM2.7 billion

    TM unit TM Technology Services was designated universal service provider for Part 1 of the SALAM (Sambungan Kabel Dasar Laut MADANI) project, covering design, manufacture, installation, testing and commissioning of four submarine cable segments at an approved maximum cost of RM2.7 billion. Work is due to start on Dec 15, with phased completion by 1Q2030; TM is still reviewing the terms.

    TMSource: The Star ↗
  4. Results

    Dialog's 1Q net profit rises 18% to RM165.08 million

    Dialog Group's net profit for the first quarter of FY2027, ended Sept 30, was RM165.08 million, up from RM140.02 million a year earlier, while revenue rose about 20% to RM787.04 million from RM657 million.

    DIALOGSource: The Star ↗
  5. Contract

    Sum Technology wins Philippine aviation hangar job, provisionally RM30 million

    Sum Technology accepted a letter of award from a Philippine aviation client for a design-and-build mechanical works package for a proposed aircraft hangar. The contract is provisionally worth about RM30 million, subject to the final bill of quantities, and start and completion dates have not been fixed.

    Source: The Star ↗
  6. IPO

    Big Caring debuts on the Main Market tomorrow at RM1.08

    Big Caring Group set its final price at RM1.08 a share; its public portion was 4.31 times oversubscribed. It lists on the Main Market on Nov 19 with a market value of about RM8.0 billion.

    BCGSource: The Edge ↗
