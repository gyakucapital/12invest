KLCI snaps five-day slide to close at 1,588.03; Zetrix AI says it cannot repay RM2b sukuk as KEB debuts today

Mon, 16 Nov 202612Invest
FBM KLCI · Mon, 16 Nov 2026 close
1,588.03
Change
+5.44（+0.34%）

Bargain hunting in selected blue chips helped the index end a five-day losing streak, trading between 1,572.55 and 1,593.02 before closing higher; market breadth stayed negative, however, and turnover was lower than on Friday. Source: Free Malaysia Today (Bernama) ↗

  1. Global

    Wall Street mixed on Friday as Nasdaq falls 0.91% amid Nvidia turmoil

    The Dow edged up 50.46 points (+0.10%) to 52,347.55, while the S&P 500 fell 0.40% to 7,561.67 and the Nasdaq dropped 0.91% to 25,136.27. CFO Colette Kress is interim CEO after Jensen Huang's resignation, and Nvidia shares lost 12.75% over the week.

    Source: TheStreet ↗
  2. Policy

    Melaka polls: BN wins 21 seats, PH 4 and PN 3

    The Election Commission said Barisan Nasional won 21 of the 28 seats in the 16th Melaka state election, Pakatan Harapan 4 and Perikatan Nasional 3, on a 71.77% turnout (493,573 voters).

    Source: Bernama ↗
  3. Corporate

    Zetrix AI says it cannot repay its RM2 billion sukuk for now

    In its disclosure to Bursa, Zetrix AI said it is currently unable to repay the full RM2 billion outstanding under its sukuk programme, is negotiating covenant changes with sukukholders and has proposed pledging up to 10% of net assets as security. It withdrew its first interim dividend for 2026 and expects the suspension of its JPJ collection-agent role to cut group revenue by about 15%; the stock closed unchanged at 6 sen on Friday.

    ZETRIXSource: The Edge ↗
  4. IPO

    KEB debuts on ACE today; MyDCD tomorrow and Big Caring on Thursday

    Property developer KEB, priced at 35 sen a share, lists on the ACE Market today. MyDCD (56 sen), whose public portion was 36.46 times oversubscribed, lists tomorrow, and Big Caring, priced at RM1.08, debuts on the Main Market on Nov 19.

    KEBMYDCDBCGSource: The Star ↗
  5. Results

    Hartalega's price-driven 2Q seen hard to sustain; HLIB cuts to sell

    Hartalega's 1HFY2027 net profit of RM102.83 million was more than triple a year earlier, but analysts see the higher-ASP second quarter as hard to repeat. HLIB cut its target price to 80 sen from 97 sen, while CIMB IB kept RM1.12 and expects a q-o-q profit decline in the third quarter. The ASP is now about US$22 per 1,000 pieces, roughly 15% below US$26.70 in 2Q.

    HARTASource: The Edge ↗
  6. Market

    Analyst: KLCI likely to consolidate between 1,580 and 1,600 this week

    Rakuten Trade expects the index to consolidate in a 1,580–1,600 range, with bargain hunting likely to emerge gradually as valuations turn attractive. In focus: Malaysia's 3Q GDP, October inflation and trade data, China's industrial production and retail sales, and the Fed's meeting minutes. The KLCI lost 33.99 points last week as weekly turnover fell to RM7.70 billion.

    Source: Bernama ↗
