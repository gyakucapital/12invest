综指开盘挫 6.26 点，盘中一度下探 1,556.51 点的日低，所幸尾盘银行权值股获买盘承接，马银行、大众银行及兴业银行走高，助指数收复大部分失地，仅微跌收市。 来源: Bernama（via Free Malaysia Today） ↗

  1. IPO

    Big Caring 今日登陆主板，招股价 RM1.08

    大马最大药房连锁 Big Caring 集团今日在主板挂牌，招股价每股 RM1.08，公众部分获 4.31 倍超额认购（1.4764 亿股吸引 7.8375 亿股申购）。此次 IPO 预计筹资 RM2.03 billion，其中 RM637.8 million 来自新股；以扩大后 73.8 亿股计，上市市值约 RM7.97 billion。

    BCG来源： The Edge ↗
  2. 业绩

    高松第三季净利增 131% 至 RM87.41 million，派息 2.5 仙

    高松截至 9 月 30 日的第三季净利达 RM87.41 million，去年同期为 RM37.83 million；营收按年增 53% 至 RM671.12 million。公司归功于平均售价及销量上升、生产效率改善，但令吉兑美元走强带来部分汇兑冲击；宣布派发每股 2.5 仙中期股息，12 月 18 日支付。

    KOSSAN来源： The Edge ↗
  3. 业绩

    子母牌第三季净利增 26%，派息 40 仙

    子母牌第三季净利按年增 26.3% 至 RM40.47 million，营收增 6.6% 至 RM399.26 million；首九个月净利增 34.9% 至 RM108.6 million。公司宣布第二次中期股息每股 40 仙，但预计乳制品原料价格在第四季及 2027 年将继续上涨，对赚幅构成压力。

    DLADY来源： The Edge ↗
  4. 业绩

    TSH 资源第三季净利跌 68.5%，受印尼 RM42.5 million 罚款拖累

    TSH 资源截至 9 月 30 日的第三季净利为 RM17.78 million，较去年同期的 RM56.41 million 下滑 68.5%，主要因印尼当局开出 RM42.5 million 罚款。

    TSH来源： Lumi News ↗
  5. 业绩

    百乐镇第三季净利减 7%，九个月房产销售跌 19%

    百乐镇第三季营收增至 RM298.2 million（去年同期 RM242.53 million），但净利从 RM25.02 million 降至 RM23.19 million，本季未派息。首九个月房产销售 RM626 million，低于去年同期的 RM774 million；新推项目总发展值从 RM716 million 降至 RM260 million。

    PARAMON来源： The Edge ↗
  6. 合约

    CBH Engineering 获吉兰丹太阳能电站工程，值 RM50.98 million

    CBH Engineering 获一家未具名的太阳能工程公司颁发吉兰丹太阳能光伏项目的工程、采购与建造（EPC）合约，涵盖电站并网设施及相关工程，价值 RM50.98 million。工程已于 9 月 4 日展开，预定 2027 年 8 月 31 日完成。

    CBHB来源： The Star ↗
  7. 大市

    公积金局第三季投资收入减 13% 至 RM25.8 billion

    雇员公积金局第三季投资收入按年减少 13% 至 RM25.8 billion，降幅约 RM4 billion，主要受股市及海外投资表现疲弱拖累，市场关注今年股息能否维持去年的 6.15%。

    来源： The Star ↗

KLCI edges down 0.03% to 1,566.16; Big Caring debuts today as Kossan's 3Q profit more than doubles

Thu, 19 Nov 202612Invest
FBM KLCI · Thu, 19 Nov 2026 close
1,566.16
Change
-0.57（-0.03%）

The KLCI opened 6.26 points lower and slid to an intraday low of 1,556.51, but late buying in banking heavyweights such as Maybank, Public Bank and RHB Bank helped it recover most of the losses and close only marginally lower. Source: Bernama（via Free Malaysia Today） ↗

  1. IPO

    Big Caring debuts on the Main Market today at RM1.08

    Big Caring Group, Malaysia's largest pharmacy chain, lists on the Main Market today at an IPO price of RM1.08 a share. Its public portion was 4.31 times oversubscribed (applications for 783.75 million shares against 147.64 million on offer). The IPO is expected to raise RM2.03 billion, of which RM637.8 million comes from new shares, and its market value at listing is about RM7.97 billion based on an enlarged 7.38 billion shares.

    BCGSource: The Edge ↗
  2. Results

    Kossan's 3Q net profit jumps 131% to RM87.41 million, pays 2.5 sen

    Kossan's net profit for the third quarter ended Sept 30 rose to RM87.41 million from RM37.83 million a year earlier, while revenue grew 53% to RM671.12 million. It cited higher average selling prices, higher volumes and better production efficiency, partly offset by forex effects from a stronger ringgit, and declared an interim dividend of 2.5 sen payable on Dec 18.

    KOSSANSource: The Edge ↗
  3. Results

    Dutch Lady's 3Q net profit up 26%, declares 40 sen dividend

    Dutch Lady's third-quarter net profit rose 26.3% year-on-year to RM40.47 million on revenue up 6.6% to RM399.26 million; nine-month net profit grew 34.9% to RM108.6 million. It declared a second interim dividend of 40 sen a share but expects dairy raw material prices to keep rising in 4Q and into 2027, pressuring margins.

    DLADYSource: The Edge ↗
  4. Results

    TSH Resources' 3Q net profit falls 68.5% on RM42.5 million Indonesian fine

    TSH Resources' net profit for the third quarter ended Sept 30 fell 68.5% to RM17.78 million from RM56.41 million a year earlier, mainly due to a RM42.5 million fine imposed by Indonesian authorities.

    TSHSource: Lumi News ↗
  5. Results

    Paramount's 3Q net profit down 7%; nine-month property sales fall 19%

    Paramount Corp's third-quarter revenue rose to RM298.2 million from RM242.53 million, but net profit eased to RM23.19 million from RM25.02 million, and no dividend was declared. Nine-month property sales were RM626 million versus RM774 million a year earlier, and the GDV of new launches fell to RM260 million from RM716 million.

    PARAMONSource: The Edge ↗
  6. Contract

    CBH Engineering wins RM50.98 million Kelantan solar plant job

    CBH Engineering secured an EPC contract worth RM50.98 million from an unnamed solar engineering company for a solar PV project in Kelantan, covering the plant's interconnection facility and related works. Work started on Sept 4 and is scheduled for completion on Aug 31, 2027.

    CBHBSource: The Star ↗
  7. Market

    EPF's 3Q investment income falls 13% to RM25.8 billion

    The EPF's investment income for the third quarter fell 13% year-on-year to RM25.8 billion, a drop of about RM4 billion, dragged down by weaker equities and international investments, raising questions over whether this year's dividend can match last year's 6.15%.

    Source: The Star ↗
