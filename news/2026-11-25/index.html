KLCI edges up 0.61 of a point to 1,562.29; IOIPG REIT lists today, PETRONAS Chemicals swings to 3Q profit

Wed, 25 Nov 202612Invest

1,562.29
Change
+0.61（+0.03%）

The KLCI opened lower on Wednesday, moved between 1,555.03 and 1,565.17 and closed up 0.61 of a point, bucking the regional trend. Buying in selected heavyweights in consumer products and services, plantation, and transportation and logistics lent support, though market breadth was weak with losers outnumbering gainers. Source: Focus Malaysia（Bernama） ↗

  1. Global

    Wall Street mixed overnight: Dow closes above 50,000 for the first time, Nasdaq falls 1.73%

    US investors rotated out of tech into blue chips on Tuesday (Nov 24). The Dow rose 0.86% to close above 50,000 for the first time, the S&P 500 fell 0.53% to 7,198.76 and the Nasdaq Composite dropped 1.73%, as Nvidia gave back part of the previous day's rally.

    Source: 24/7 Wall St. ↗
  2. IPO

    IOIPG REIT lists on the Main Market today, Yes Group debuts tomorrow

    IOIPG Malaysia REIT lists on the Main Market today (Nov 25) at a final price of 91 sen per unit, below the 96 sen maximum retail price. Yes Group follows on the ACE Market tomorrow (Nov 26) at 18 sen a share, after its public portion was oversubscribed 2.8 times.

    IOIPGREITYESGROUPSource: The Star ↗
  3. Results

    PETRONAS Chemicals swings to RM179 million 3Q net profit

    PETRONAS Chemicals posted a third-quarter net profit of RM179 million against a net loss of RM289 million a year earlier, as revenue rose to RM7.62 billion from RM6.79 billion. Nine-month net profit was RM994 million versus a RM1.39 billion loss. Higher Pengerang contributions lifted sales volume, offsetting softer prices, and the group expects olefins and derivatives prices to stay under pressure from regional oversupply.

    PCHEMSource: The Star ↗
  4. Corporate

    Berjaya Food exits loss-making Kenny Rogers Roasters, keeps 5% stake

    Berjaya Food's subsidiary is moving the Malaysian Kenny Rogers Roasters business into a new joint venture, held 46.5% each by UAE-based Samaya Food Investments and Vertix Holdings, with the subsidiary keeping 5%; total capital contribution is RM8.64 million. The group expects a gain of about RM4.51 million, mainly from ending lease obligations, with no material effect on net assets or gearing.

    BJFOODSource: The Star ↗
  5. IPO

    Emits oversubscribed 6.43 times, lists on ACE Market on Dec 1

    The public portion of Emits' IPO was oversubscribed 6.43 times, and the company is set to list on the ACE Market on Dec 1 at 16 sen a share. Meanwhile, the public offer of SCA Solutions (28 sen) closes tomorrow (Nov 26) and EMPG's (28 sen) closes on Dec 2.

    EMITSSCAEMPGSource: The Star ↗
  6. Market

    Tonight: US 3Q GDP and October PCE inflation data

    The US releases third-quarter GDP and PCE inflation data tonight (8.30am ET, Nov 25), which markets will use to gauge the Fed rate outlook; US markets are closed on Thursday for Thanksgiving. Chip stocks fell overnight, with Micron down 11.01% and Nvidia down 5.86%.

    Source: Stocktitan ↗
