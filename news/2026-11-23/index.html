  1. 环球

    美股上周五重挫：纳指跌 5.3%，标普 500 跌入调整区

    美股上周五（11 月 20 日）全面下跌，标普 500 指数跌 3.7% 至 6,910.39 点，较 10 月 6 日纪录高位回落逾 10%，正式进入调整区；纳斯达克指数大跌 5.3% 至 22,617.73 点，抹去今年全部涨幅，道指跌 1.9% 至 48,147.33 点。费城半导体指数挫 10.7%，辉达跌 14%，市场对 AI 估值与巨额资本开支能否回本的疑虑加深，日经及韩国综指当天亦跌逾 5%。

    来源： CEO Economy ↗
  2. 大市

    本周马股料谨慎交投，市场关注美国第三季 GDP 及 PCE 数据

    Rakuten Trade 预计本周马股交投谨慎，焦点在周三公布的美国第三季 GDP 第二次预估及 10 月 PCE 通胀数据，以及美联储利率走向；美国周四感恩节休市，交投转淡或加剧波动。综指上周累跌 26.08 点至 1,556.51 点，全周成交额增至 RM14.12 billion。

    来源： Bernama ↗
  3. 大市

    令吉上周兑美元走软至 4.1105，本周料略偏强

    令吉兑美元上周五收 4.1105/1150，较前一周的 4.0845/0890 走软，兑新元、泰铢等东盟货币亦走低，但兑英镑及欧元走强。分析员预计本周令吉兑美元在 4.09 至 4.10 之间波动，市场关注 11 月 25 日公布的美国 10 月 PCE 物价指数。

    来源： The Star（Bernama） ↗
  4. IPO

    本周三只新股挂牌：Impact Capital 明日打头阵，IOIPG REIT 周三上市

    Impact Capital 明日（11 月 24 日）以每股 22 仙登陆 ACE 市场，公众部分获 139.69 倍超额认购；IOIPG Malaysia REIT 以每单位 91 仙定价，11 月 25 日在主板挂牌，公众部分认购率为 0.59 倍；Yes Group 则于 11 月 26 日以 18 仙上市。Emits 今日抽签，订于 12 月 1 日上市；SCA Solutions 申购于 11 月 26 日截止。

    7-Eleven 大马第三季净利增至 RM6 million，营收增 17.9%

    马联工业（MUI）与 Tencent Cloud International 签署非约束性、非独家谅解备忘录，探讨云端、数据及人工智能合作，任何项目须另签正式协议，预计不会对下一财年盈利构成重大影响。集团截至 6 月 30 日财年净亏损扩大至 RM153.5 million，股价上周五平收 3.5 仙。

KLCI falls 7.46 points to 1,549.05; Wall Street slumps as Nasdaq falls 5.3%, three IPOs list this week

Change

The KLCI opened 9.07 points lower today, moved between 1,544.96 and 1,555.25, and closed down 7.46 points. Persistent selling in utilities heavyweights, with Tenaga Nasional, YTL Power International and YTL Corporation all lower, weighed on the market. Source: Free Malaysia Today（Bernama） ↗

  1. Global

    Wall Street slumps Friday: Nasdaq falls 5.3%, S&P 500 enters correction

    US stocks sold off on Friday (Nov 20). The S&P 500 fell 3.7% to 6,910.39, more than 10% below its Oct 6 record, putting it in a correction; the Nasdaq dropped 5.3% to 22,617.73, wiping out its gain for the year, and the Dow lost 1.9% to 48,147.33. The Philadelphia semiconductor index fell 10.7% and Nvidia 14% as doubts grew over AI valuations and spending, while Japan's Nikkei and South Korea's Kospi each fell more than 5% that day.

    Source: CEO Economy ↗
  2. Market

    Bursa seen trading cautiously this week as investors await US GDP and PCE data

    Rakuten Trade expects cautious trading on Bursa this week, with attention on Wednesday's second estimate of US third-quarter GDP and October PCE inflation, plus the Fed rate outlook; the US Thanksgiving holiday on Thursday may thin trading and add to volatility. The KLCI fell 26.08 points last week to 1,556.51, while weekly turnover rose to RM14.12 billion.

    Source: Bernama ↗
  3. Market

    Ringgit weakens to 4.1105 against the dollar last week, seen slightly firmer this week

    The ringgit closed Friday at 4.1105/1150 against the US dollar, weaker than 4.0845/0890 a week earlier, and also fell against Asean currencies including the Singapore dollar and baht, though it firmed against the pound and euro. An analyst expects USD/MYR to trade between 4.09 and 4.10 this week, with focus on US October PCE data due Nov 25.

    Source: The Star（Bernama） ↗
  4. IPO

    Three listings this week: Impact Capital debuts tomorrow, IOIPG REIT on Wednesday

    Impact Capital lists on the ACE Market tomorrow (Nov 24) at 22 sen a share after its public portion was oversubscribed 139.69 times; IOIPG Malaysia REIT, priced at 91 sen a unit, lists on the Main Market on Nov 25 after its public tranche was 0.59 times subscribed; and Yes Group lists on Nov 26 at 18 sen. Emits holds its balloting today ahead of a Dec 1 listing, while SCA Solutions' application period closes on Nov 26.

    IMPACTIOIPGREITYESGROUPEMITSSCASource: rates.my ↗
  5. Results

    7-Eleven Malaysia's 3Q net profit rises to RM6 million, revenue up 17.9%

    7-Eleven Malaysia's net profit for the third quarter ended Sept 30 was RM6 million versus RM2 million a year earlier, and revenue rose 17.9% to RM938.4 million on 121 net new stores and public holidays that lifted average daily sales per store. Nine-month net profit was still down 40.6% at RM19 million, on revenue up 12.9% to RM2.66 billion.

    SEMSource: Business Today ↗
  6. Results

    AEON Malaysia's 3Q net profit falls 35.7% to RM9 million

    AEON Co (M)'s third-quarter net profit fell to RM9 million from RM14 million a year earlier, while revenue edged up 1.6% to RM1.01 billion, with retail revenue up 1% to RM811 million and property management services up 3.9% to RM200 million. Nine-month net profit rose 12.8% to RM107.2 million.

    AEONSource: Business Today ↗
  7. Corporate

    MUI signs non-binding MOU with Tencent Cloud to explore cloud and AI collaboration

    Malayan United Industries signed a non-binding, non-exclusive memorandum of understanding with Tencent Cloud International to explore cloud, data and AI cooperation; any project requires separate definitive agreements, and the MOU is not expected to materially affect earnings next financial year. The group's net loss for the year ended June 30 widened to RM153.5 million, and its shares closed unchanged at 3.5 sen on Friday.

    MUIINDSource: The Edge Malaysia ↗
