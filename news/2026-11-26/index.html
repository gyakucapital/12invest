马股涨 8.69 点收 1,570.98 点；Yes Group 今日挂牌，恒源第三季净利飙至 RM557.95 million

+8.69（+0.55%）

涨 412 / 跌 703 / 平 408

33.5 亿股，RM3.29 billion

综指周四高开后在 1,563.15 至 1,584.96 点之间上落，终场涨 8.69 点，跟随区域股市走高，金融服务及消费产品与服务领域部分重量股获买盘支撑。不过大市跌股仍多于涨股，成交额则从前一日的 RM2.36 billion 增至 RM3.29 billion。 来源: Focus Malaysia（Bernama） ↗

  1. IPO

    Yes Group 今日登陆 ACE 市场，SCA Solutions 公开申购今日截止

    Yes Group 今日（11 月 26 日）以每股 18 仙在 ACE 市场挂牌，其公众部分获 2.8 倍超额认购。SCA Solutions（每股 28 仙）的公开申购于今日截止，订于 11 月 30 日抽签，12 月 9 日上市。

    YESGROUPSCA来源： Dagang News ↗
  2. 业绩

    恒源炼油第三季净利跃升至 RM557.95 million

    恒源（Hengyuan）截至 9 月 30 日第三季净利 RM557.95 million，去年同期仅 RM21.04 million；营收增长 51.4% 至 RM5.48 billion。首九个月净利 RM1.68 billion，去年同期净亏 RM332.65 million。公司指各主要产品售价走高带动表现，并预计西亚局势及俄罗斯炼油受干扰将令燃料供应维持紧张。

    HENGYUAN来源： The Star（Bernama） ↗
  3. 业绩

    马电讯第三季净利跌 28% 至 RM494 million，派息 10 仙

    马电讯（TM）第三季净利 RM494 million，去年同期为 RM686.3 million，主要因去年同期有一次性投资公允值收益；营收微增 0.3% 至 RM3 billion。公司宣布派发第三次中期股息 10 仙，维持全年营收低单位数增长的指引，并把资本开支占营收比例下调至 16% 至 18%。

    TM来源： The Star ↗
  4. 业绩

    双威第三季营收增 18%，建筑订单创 RM12.45 billion 新高

    双威（Sunway）第三季营收增 18% 至 RM3.03 billion，但净利由 RM338.14 million 降至 RM314.06 million。建筑业务今年迄今获 RM9.8 billion 新订单，超出全年 RM7 billion 至 RM9 billion 的目标，未完成订单达 RM12.45 billion；首九个月产业销售 RM3.4 billion，全年目标 RM4.2 billion。

    SUNWAY来源： The Star ↗
  5. 业绩

    Kerjaya 第三季营收下滑但净利增至 RM69.89 million

    Kerjaya Prospek 第三季净利 RM69.89 million，高于去年同期的 RM57.44 million；营收则因建筑工程进度放缓，由 RM566.25 million 降至 RM396.07 million。公司派发 3.5 仙中期股息，截至 9 月 30 日未完成建筑订单为 RM6.1 billion。

    KERJAYA来源： The Star ↗
  6. 业绩

    安联银行次季净利 RM208.47 million，派息 11.88 仙

    安联银行（Alliance Bank）截至 9 月 30 日的 2026 财年次季净利 RM208.47 million，去年同期 RM206.56 million；营收由 RM626.23 million 增至 RM648.5 million。上半年净利 RM456.78 million，宣布派发首次中期股息 11.88 仙，并维持全年指引。

    ABMB来源： The Star ↗
  7. 业绩

    大马皇帽第三季净利增 8.7% 至 RM112 million

    大马皇帽（Carlsberg Malaysia）第三季净利增 8.7% 至 RM112 million，营收增 1% 至 RM589.2 million，主要因经销商在 10 月系统转换前提前囤货；公司派发 27 仙中期股息。管理层预计第四季出货量将随经销商库存回落而受影响，消费情绪料保持谨慎。

    CARLSBG来源： The Star ↗

KLCI rises 8.69 points to 1,570.98; Yes Group lists today, Hengyuan 3Q profit soars to RM557.95 million

Thu, 26 Nov 202612Invest
FBM KLCI · Thu, 26 Nov 2026 close
1,570.98
Change
+8.69（+0.55%）

The KLCI opened higher on Thursday, moved between 1,563.15 and 1,584.96 and closed up 8.69 points, tracking upbeat regional markets as buying in selected financial services and consumer products and services heavyweights lent support. Losers still outnumbered gainers, while turnover rose to RM3.29 billion from Wednesday's RM2.36 billion. Source: Focus Malaysia（Bernama） ↗

  1. IPO

    Yes Group debuts on the ACE Market today; SCA Solutions public offer closes today

    Yes Group lists on the ACE Market today (Nov 26) at 18 sen a share, after its public portion was oversubscribed 2.8 times. The public offer of SCA Solutions (28 sen) closes today, with balloting on Nov 30 and listing on Dec 9.

    YESGROUPSCASource: Dagang News ↗
  2. Results

    Hengyuan's 3Q net profit jumps to RM557.95 million

    Hengyuan posted net profit of RM557.95 million for the third quarter ended Sept 30, up from RM21.04 million a year earlier, as revenue rose 51.4% to RM5.48 billion. Nine-month net profit was RM1.68 billion against a RM332.65 million loss. Higher selling prices across key products drove the result, and the refiner expects fuel supplies to stay tight amid West Asia tensions and disruptions to Russian refining.

    HENGYUANSource: The Star（Bernama） ↗
  3. Results

    TM's 3Q net profit falls 28% to RM494 million; 10 sen dividend

    Telekom Malaysia's third-quarter net profit fell to RM494 million from RM686.3 million, mainly because the year-ago quarter included a one-off fair value gain on an investment; revenue edged up 0.3% to RM3 billion. TM declared a third interim dividend of 10 sen, kept its low single-digit revenue growth guidance and cut its capex-to-revenue guidance to 16%-18%.

    TMSource: The Star ↗
  4. Results

    Sunway 3Q revenue up 18%, construction order book at record RM12.45 billion

    Sunway's third-quarter revenue rose 18% to RM3.03 billion, while net profit eased to RM314.06 million from RM338.14 million. Its construction arm has won RM9.8 billion in new orders so far this year, above its RM7 billion to RM9 billion full-year target, lifting the outstanding order book to RM12.45 billion. Nine-month property sales reached RM3.4 billion against a RM4.2 billion target.

    SUNWAYSource: The Star ↗
  5. Results

    Kerjaya's 3Q net profit rises to RM69.89 million despite lower revenue

    Kerjaya Prospek's third-quarter net profit rose to RM69.89 million from RM57.44 million, though revenue fell to RM396.07 million from RM566.25 million on slower construction progress. It declared a 3.5 sen interim dividend and had an outstanding construction order book of RM6.1 billion as at Sept 30.

    KERJAYASource: The Star ↗
  6. Results

    Alliance Bank 2Q net profit at RM208.47 million; 11.88 sen dividend

    Alliance Bank's net profit for the second quarter of FY2027 (ended Sept 30) was RM208.47 million versus RM206.56 million a year earlier, with revenue up to RM648.5 million from RM626.23 million. First-half net profit was RM456.78 million; the bank declared a first interim dividend of 11.88 sen and kept its full-year guidance.

    ABMBSource: The Star ↗
  7. Results

    Carlsberg Malaysia's 3Q net profit up 8.7% to RM112 million

    Carlsberg Malaysia's third-quarter net profit rose 8.7% to RM112 million and revenue gained 1% to RM589.2 million, helped by distributors stocking up ahead of an October system migration; it declared a 27 sen interim dividend. Management expects fourth-quarter sell-in to be hit as distributor inventory normalises, with consumer sentiment staying cautious.

    CARLSBGSource: The Star ↗
