KLCI falls 0.77% to 1,604.07; US tech rebounds as Bursa orders Zetrix AI to pay overdue dividend by Nov 23

Tue, 10 Nov 202612Invest
FBM KLCI · Tue, 10 Nov 2026 close
1,604.07
Change
-12.51（-0.77%）

On the first trading day after the Deepavali holiday, selling in banking and data-centre-related heavyweights pulled the index lower, with decliners outnumbering gainers. Source: Free Malaysia Today ↗

  1. Global

    Wall Street rebounds strongly on Monday as oil slips on US-Iran deal hopes

    Dip buyers returned after last week's AI sell-off: the Nasdaq-100 closed 3.40% higher and the Russell 2000 3.19%, with other major indexes up more than 2%, while Nvidia fell amid an internal board investigation. Trump played down the chance of fresh strikes on Iran, and Brent fell 3.63% to US$94.75 a barrel in early trade.

    Source: TheStreet ↗
  2. Corporate

    Bursa orders Zetrix AI to pay overdue dividend by Nov 23

    In a Nov 6 letter, Bursa Malaysia directed Zetrix AI to pay its FY2025 final dividend of 2.89 sen a share immediately and no later than Nov 23, after repeated delays since September. MARC has cut its RM2 billion sukuk programme to D-IS (default) after the company failed to repay a RM25 million maturing bond.

    ZETRIXSource: DagangNews ↗
  3. IPO

    SKA Capital debuts on ACE tomorrow; KEB and MyDCD list next week

    SKA Capital (43 sen a share), whose public portion was 80.42 times oversubscribed, lists on the ACE Market on Nov 11, followed by KEB on Nov 16 and MyDCD on Nov 17.

    SKACAPKEBMYDCDSource: The Star ↗
  4. Results

    Results week: Hartalega on Thursday, MR DIY and Carlsberg on Friday

    Hartalega reports its 2QFY2027 results on Thursday, which may be weaker on margin compression; Heineken Malaysia and F&N report the same day, and MR DIY and Carlsberg on Friday.

    HARTAHEIMF&NMRDIYCARLSBGSource: The Edge ↗
  5. Corporate

    West River shifts to higher-margin jobs with an order book of about RM200m

    M&E engineering firm West River has an order book of RM195 million to RM200 million and is bidding for TNB solar-farm substation and grid-connection work while targeting data centre and semiconductor plant jobs. Its MD expects growth of at least 30% in FY26 and 30% to 50% in FY27, with no dividends for at least another year.

    WESTRVRSource: The Star ↗
  6. Policy

    Melaka early voting starts today; industrial production and palm oil data due

    Early voting for the Melaka state election begins today, with 79 candidates contesting 28 seats on polling day, Nov 14. DOSM releases September industrial production today and MPOB publishes October palm oil supply and demand data.

    Source: The Edge ↗
  7. Market

    Analyst sees KLCI trading between 1,600 and 1,630 this week

    Rakuten Trade expects the index to hover between 1,600 and 1,630, with selective buying cushioning dips, as markets watch US October consumer and producer price data. The KLCI edged up only 0.98 points last week while weekly turnover rose to RM14.43 billion.

    Source: Bernama ↗
